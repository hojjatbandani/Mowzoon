Free plan and Mowzoon Pro

Free plan

The Free plan includes expense tracking and budgets, 10 receipt scans and 15 AI actions each month, and up to 3 recurring bills. Monthly allowances reset at the start of each month. We may change these allowances; we will show the current ones in the app.

Mowzoon Pro

Mowzoon Pro is an optional subscription, billed monthly or yearly, that adds unlimited receipt scans and recurring bills, much more AI use and cloud backup. Pro AI use is generous but subject to fair use, so the service stays fast and reliable for everyone; if you reach the fair-use level in a month, AI features pause until the next month.
