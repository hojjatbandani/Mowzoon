Delete your Mowzoon account

You can ask us to delete your Mowzoon account and the data linked to it at any time. Here is how, what we delete, and how long it takes.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Before you start: cancel Mowzoon Pro

Deleting your account does not cancel a Mowzoon Pro subscription — Google Play or Apple bills it. Cancel it first so you are not charged again.

  • Android: open the Google Play Store app → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Mowzoon → Cancel subscription.
  • iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mowzoon → Cancel Subscription.

Request deletion

Send us a deletion request from the email address you use to sign in to Mowzoon. The form below prepares the email for you; you can also write to privacy@mowzoon.app with the subject “Delete my Mowzoon account”.

This opens your email app with the request filled in — just press send. If you didn’t sign in to Mowzoon, you have no account with us: see “Data on your phone” below.

Your email app should now be open with the request. After you send it, we will reply to confirm.

Data on your phone

Your expenses, budgets, bills and settings are stored on your phone, not on our servers. To delete them:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Mowzoon → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • iPhone: delete the app from your Home Screen.

Signing out of Mowzoon keeps this data on your phone.

What we delete

  • your Mowzoon sign-in account (Firebase Authentication);
  • your email address and sign-in details stored by Mowzoon;
  • your cloud backups;
  • the subscription and usage records linked to your account.

What may be kept

  • Google Play and Apple keep their own records of your purchases under their policies.
  • We may keep records we are required to keep by law, such as for accounting or fraud prevention, only for as long as required.
  • Anonymous analytics that are not linked to your name or email are not affected.

How long it takes

We confirm your request within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from the same email address, to make sure the request is really yours.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@mowzoon.app. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
