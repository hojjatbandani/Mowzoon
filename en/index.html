How it works

From payday to payday, in three steps

Mowzoon plans by salary cycle, not by calendar month — so whether you are paid on the 25th or the 1st, your budget starts when your salary lands.

  1. 1

    Set up in a minute

    Choose your country, add your salary and payday, and your budgets and bills if you like.

  2. 2

    Log spending your way

    Talk, type or scan a receipt. Mowzoon files each expense under the right category.

  3. 3

    Know what’s safe to spend

    See today’s safe-to-spend amount, the days to payday, and AI insights on where your money goes.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything people ask about Mowzoon, the AI budget and expense tracker for the GCC.

What is Mowzoon?

Mowzoon is an AI-powered budget and expense tracker for people in the GCC. It helps you record expenses by voice, text or receipt scan, plan a monthly budget around your salary, keep track of recurring bills and saving goals, and get AI insights on where your money goes. It works in Arabic and English.

Is Mowzoon available in Saudi Arabia, UAE and Oman?

Yes. Mowzoon is built for Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Oman, Kuwait, Qatar and Bahrain, with each country's currency and Arabic-first support. It is available for Android on Google Play, and an iPhone version is coming soon.

Can Mowzoon scan receipts?

Yes. Take a photo of a receipt and Mowzoon reads the total, the date and the shop, then files the expense for you. With smart receipt read, AI also reads every item on the receipt. The free plan includes 10 receipt scans a month, and Mowzoon Pro removes the limit.

How does Mowzoon use AI?

In four ways: Talk to Mowzoon, to log spending and ask about your money by voice or text; AI Insights, a summary of your salary cycle with suggestions on where to save; the AI Budget Builder, which proposes a limit for every category; and smart receipt read. Every change the AI makes is shown to you and can be undone.

Can I create a monthly budget?

Yes. Set limits for food, transport, shopping, bills, entertainment and more, or let the AI Budget Builder suggest them from your salary, bills and saving goal. Mowzoon follows your salary cycle, so your month starts on payday, and it shows how much is safe to spend each day. You get an alert at 80% and 100% of a category limit.

Can I track recurring bills and subscriptions?

Yes. Add rent, electricity, internet, mobile, subscriptions and installments with their due dates. Mowzoon reminds you before each one is due and sets the money aside in your plan. The free plan includes 3 recurring bills, and Mowzoon Pro makes them unlimited.

Which currencies does Mowzoon support?

Saudi riyal (SAR), UAE dirham (AED), Omani rial (OMR), Kuwaiti dinar (KWD), Qatari riyal (QAR), Bahraini dinar (BHD) and US dollar (USD). You choose a main currency, and spending in another currency is converted with exchange rates you set.

Is Mowzoon free?

Yes. The free plan includes expense tracking, budgets, 10 receipt scans and 15 AI actions a month, and up to 3 recurring bills. Mowzoon Pro, a monthly or yearly subscription, adds unlimited receipt scans and recurring bills, much more AI, and cloud backup. Google Play shows the price in your local currency.

Is my financial data secure?

Your expenses and budgets are stored on your phone. Signing in is optional and only needed for cloud backup. When you use an AI feature, only what that answer needs is sent over an encrypted connection; it is not stored on our servers and never used for advertising. You can delete your account and data at any time.
